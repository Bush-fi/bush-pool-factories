# StablePoolFactory — Gas Characteristics

Numbers below are taken verbatim from the copied Hardhat gas snapshots in
`test/gas/.hardhat-snapshots/` (generated by `test/gas/StablePoolBenchmark.test.ts`), which
were produced by running the actual Solidity contracts. They are not
estimates. "Warm slots" means the relevant storage slots (token balances, allowances, etc.)
were already non-zero/touched in a prior transaction in the same test (the realistic steady
state); "cold slots" means the first touch in that context (higher gas due to EVM cold-access
surcharges).

No dedicated "pool creation" (factory `create()` call) benchmark exists in the copied gas
suite — the closest proxies are the `initialize` figures below, which include the first
liquidity deposit into a freshly created pool.

## Takeaways

- A rate provider ("WithRate" scenarios) adds roughly 15k–30k gas to most operations, from
  the extra external `getRate()` call(s) and rate-scaling math.
- Routing a swap through the `BatchRouter` (vs. calling the pool/Vault swap path directly)
  adds a modest, consistent overhead (roughly 10k–30k gas) across scenarios.
- ERC4626 buffer-liquidity swaps are the most expensive category, since they combine an
  ERC4626 `deposit`/`redeem`/`mint`/`withdraw` with the underlying stable swap; having warm
  buffer liquidity available cuts gas by roughly 65k–75k versus routing through the
  underlying market with no buffer.
